What the group covers

Real-world skills with a strong community feel.

New members are welcome. The focus is practical training, responsible culture, and the kind of readiness that helps families and neighbors.

01

Preparedness

Kit planning, calm decision-making, communication habits, and readiness before stressful conditions arrive.

02

Fieldcraft

Outdoor awareness, navigation mindset, weather planning, movement safety, and practical risk reduction.

03

Medical

First-aid priorities, training events, and the discipline to be useful while waiting for professional help.

04

Food and water

Water procurement, camp cooking, basic food security concepts, and self-reliance habits.

05

Community

All skill levels welcome, family friendly, and built around stronger tomorrows instead of empty hype.

06

Responsible culture

Training that pairs preparedness with lawful ownership, safety, humility, and clear judgment.
